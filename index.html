Designer's corrections, revision 2

  • Kitchen: the whole refrigerator wall is now floor-to-ceiling oak cabinetry with an integrated fridge and oven column. The island runs to the far wall as drawn (227 cm), with the four stools in their plotted positions.
  • Finishes: herringbone oak parquet in all living spaces, modern rustic palette (natural oak, light stone, matte black, linen). Oak slat cladding behind the TV, a dusty-pink fluted panel behind the master bed.
  • Children's room: pastel mint walls with a dotted wallpaper on the long wall, pink and mint bedding.
  • Light: LED backlighting on the entrance mirror and both bathroom mirrors; a lit display cabinet with glass shelves.
  • Utility: overhead cabinets above the washing machine extended to the ceiling. The guest-bath vanity now runs the full length of the wall from the bathtub to the corner.
  • Balcony: artificial grass, two rattan armchairs with a side table along the railing, and a 1.4 m trampoline with a safety net at the north end.
  • Still assumed: 2.75 m clear height; door heights from the block names; the safe-room window at sill 100, 100 cm high; a 15 cm curb with glass railing. The building outside the apartment is not drawn on the plan and not modelled.
